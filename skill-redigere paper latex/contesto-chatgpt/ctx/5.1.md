## Verdicts and causal language (RULES.md)

- Hypothesis verdicts use the paper's Table 13 wording only: **right sign, not significant** · **rejected, opposite sign** · **not supported**. Never "no effect" or "confirmed".
- About the gap or hype, never write *effect, impact, causes, leads to, reduces, increases, drives*. Write **is associated with**, **goes with**.

## Contenuto (l'unico materiale tuo, insieme ai numeri e alle fonti qui sotto)
1. **H1: right sign, not significant**: *β1 = −0.089, p = 0.188, odds ratio 0.915, interval includes zero*
   In the baseline logit the gap coefficient is −0.089 (p = 0.188): a 2.7-fold longer gap goes with 8.5% lower odds of exit. The sign matches H1, but the data cannot tell a small penalty from none.
2. **H2: opposite sign, not significant**: *β1 = −0.071, β3 = −0.056, p = 0.344, slope steeper in hot markets*
   With hype interactions, β1 = −0.071 (p = 0.294) and β3 = −0.056 (p = 0.344). The gap slope gets steeper, not flatter, as hype rises, which is the opposite of H2. β3 is not significant.
3. **The same in probabilities**: *AME cold −0.3 pp, hot −2.1 pp (p = 0.07), hot − cold −1.9 pp (p = 0.33), LPM*
   AMEs per unit of ln gap: about zero in cold markets, −2.1 pp in hot ones (p = 0.07). The hot-minus-cold difference, −1.9 pp, is not significant (p = 0.33). The LPM gives the same reading.
4. **Predicted probabilities**: *Figure 12, cold flat about 22%, hot 26% → 20%, overlapping bands*
   Predicted exit is flat at about 22% in cold markets. In hot markets it falls from about 26% at a gap of about 2 months to about 20% at about 4½ years, but the bands overlap everywhere (Figures 11–12). (Grid ln(1 + months) = 1 to 4, i.e. 1.7 to 54 months; cold 0.220 → 0.213, hot 0.264 → 0.200: log L863–888.)
5. **Controls are larger than the gap**: *round-1 size OR 1.64, age +10%, angel group +94%, CVC +55%, VC +39%*
   Round-1 size and age at round 1 go with much higher exit odds, and angel-group, CVC and VC leads with higher odds than no identified lead. Moving across the gap IQR changes exit by less than one point.

## Sezione precedente (gia' scritta, per la continuita')
**5 Results**

Every logit coefficient is a change in log-odds. We also report the odds ratio, exp(β), and the average marginal effect (AME) in percentage points. The linear probability model coefficient is already in probability units and checks the AME.

The gap enters as ln(1 + months), so one unit multiplies 1 + months by 2.7. A 10% longer gap goes with a log-odds difference of about β/10. Magnitudes use the interquartile range of ln gap, 2.24 to 3.02, which is 8 to 19 months.

In the interacted model the gap slope is β1 + β3 · hype. β1 is the slope at average hype, and β3 is its change per standard deviation of hype. ρ1 is the group difference at a zero-month gap, outside the data.

Unconditional five-year rates give the scale: 22.2% of two-round and 15.6% of one-round startups exit, and 12.4% and 31.6% fail. A two-point change in exit is about a tenth of the two-round rate.
