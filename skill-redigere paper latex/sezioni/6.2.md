## DRAFT

Whether a startup raises a second VC round is known only after round 1. Our two-group design therefore defines R using future information relative to round-1 controls and hype.

An early failure or acquisition prevents a subsequent round. Some one-round startups are consequently classified by the outcome itself, creating a mechanical link between R and outcomes.

ρ1 mixes selection of better startups into round 2 with this mechanical link. We report it as a group difference, not as the effect of raising another round.

β1 and β3 are estimated within two-round startups and are much less affected by look-ahead. Excluding one-round startups with an exit or failure within 13 months leaves both coefficients unchanged ([[SEC:5.4]]).
